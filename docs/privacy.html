Privacy Policy

Liquid Glass for Photoshop and Illustrator, by CGSudo. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Short version

The plugins collect no personal information. Everything runs on your computer.

What the plugins do with your data

  • Your documents are read and rendered locally, inside Photoshop or Illustrator. No image, layer or document data ever leaves your computer.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • The plugins make no network requests. Links in the panel (help, website, LinkedIn) open in your browser only when you click them.
  • Looks you save are stored on your computer, in the plugin's own storage.

Purchases

If you buy the plugin on the Adobe Marketplace, the purchase is handled by Adobe and its payment provider, FastSpring, under their own privacy policies. The developer receives no payment card details.

Support emails

If you email for support, your message is used only to answer you and is never shared or sold.
