Sudo Liquid Glass - live, real Liquid Glass for Photoshop and Illustrator.

ONE INSTALLER FOR BOTH APPS
  Windows: close Photoshop and Illustrator, then double-click "Install on Windows.bat".
    It installs into every Photoshop and Illustrator it finds. Allow it when Windows asks for
    admin rights (needed for Illustrator's Plug-ins folder).
  Mac: double-click "Install on Mac.command" (Photoshop; there is no Mac Illustrator version yet).
    If macOS says it can't be opened, right-click it > Open > Open (it is a small script, not signed by Apple).

THEN
  Photoshop: restart it and open Plugins > Sudo Liquid Glass.
  Illustrator: select a shape and choose Effect > Stylize > Liquid Glass...
    Change it later: double-click Liquid Glass in the Appearance panel, or Object > Edit Liquid Glass...

NEEDS
  Photoshop 2023 (v24.2) or newer and the Creative Cloud desktop app.
  Illustrator 2026 on Windows 10 or 11 (with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, built into Windows 11).

Inside: SudoLiquidGlass.ccx is the Photoshop plugin (you can also double-click it),
LiquidGlass.aip is the Illustrator plug-in. Both use the same glass engine and settings.
